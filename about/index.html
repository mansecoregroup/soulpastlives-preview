About Carl

My work began in 1996 with counselling, then hypnosis, then past life regression. What I’ve learned is simple: people find what they’re ready to find, and the meaning is always theirs.

Most people who come to me have carried a question for years.

A fear that never made sense. A pattern that keeps returning. A place, a person or a period of history that feels strangely familiar. Or simply the sense that there may be more to them than this life.

Past life regression has fascinated me for most of my working life. Over the years I’ve learned to create the conditions and then step back: to guide without leading, and to let people look for themselves.

For some, one life is enough. Others want to go further, into what seems to come between lives. I guide both.

I don’t ask anyone to believe in past lives. Some people leave certain they remembered; others find meaning of a different kind. Both are welcome here.

I co-founded The Manse Retreat, and co-created The Manse Method® and Emotion Pathway®.

Both change the emotional response attached to past experiences, without anyone needing to talk through what happened. They’re now taught to practitioners through Manse Core Group.

Featured in Cosmopolitan, Woman & Home, The Weekend Pages, and interviewed on NTD News in New York.
