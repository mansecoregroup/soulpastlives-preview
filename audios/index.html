Guided audios

Listen at home.

Recorded journeys in my own voice, to download and keep. Use them to explore on your own, or alongside a session with me.

See the recordings

The recordings

  1. Guided regression · About 40 minutes

    A Past Life Journey

    A gentle way to look back for yourself.

    I guide you into a calm, focused state, then through a doorway towards a life that seems to be yours from another time. You notice where you are, who you are, and what that life wants you to see, then come back slowly, rested and clear.

    Coming soon

  2. Guided journey · About 40 minutes

    Meeting a Loved One

    A quiet place to be with someone you miss.

    For anyone carrying a loss. I take you to a peaceful place where you can open yourself to the presence of someone who has passed away, and say what you need to say. Some people feel a strong sense of meeting; for others it’s quieter. Either way, it’s time set aside for them, and for you.

    Coming soon

  3. Guided journey · About 35 minutes

    Meeting Your Guide

    Asking the question you’ve been carrying.

    In deep relaxation, many people meet a wise, kind presence: a guide, a teacher, or a calmer part of themselves. On this journey I take you to meet your guide, so you can ask what you most want to know and hear what comes back. What you make of who you meet is up to you.

    Coming soon

  4. Between-lives journey · About 45 minutes

    Your Soul Group

    The faces that seem to travel with you.

    In between-lives regression, people often describe returning to a group of souls they know deeply, some of them familiar from this life. On this journey I take you back to them, so you can see who is there and what you share.

    Coming soon

How it works

Each recording is an MP3 you buy through my shop on Payhip. You download it straight away, keep it, and listen as often as you like, on headphones if you can.

Before you listen

Only listen when you can safely close your eyes and relax: never while driving or using machinery. The recordings are for exploration, not a substitute for medical or psychological care. If you have epilepsy, a psychotic illness or are in crisis, please talk to your doctor first.
