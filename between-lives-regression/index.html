The space between

Between-lives regression

Beyond the end of a remembered life, people describe somewhere else. A deeper session, for when one life isn’t the whole question.

A longer, deeper session.

It usually begins as a past life regression. Then, instead of returning at the end of that life, you’re guided gently beyond it.

Into the space between.

People describe what seems to come after one life and before the next: rest, light, a sense of being met, of looking back over a life, of people they already know.

A past life session comes first.

Before a between-lives regression, we always do a past life regression, in a separate session. By then the state feels familiar, and the deeper session is easier to settle into.

The meaning is yours.

Some take it as a memory of the soul’s journey. Others as a deep inner experience they can’t easily name. I don’t tell you what to believe.

What people ask.

How is it different from past life regression?

A past life regression explores a life. A between-lives regression goes further, into what seems to come after that life ends and before another begins. It’s deeper and takes longer.

Do I need a past life regression first?

Yes. A past life regression always comes first, in a separate session. Knowing the state already makes the deeper session easier to settle into. How to prepare.

Will I have a recording?

Yes. Every session is recorded, and I send the recording to you afterwards so you can listen back.

Will I be aware?

Yes. You stay aware throughout, you can speak, and you can stop whenever you like. Most people remember the session clearly.

Do I have to believe in it?

No. Curiosity is enough. What you make of what you find is entirely your own.

Is it right for everyone?

Not always. It isn’t a substitute for medical or mental-health care. If you have a diagnosed condition or are under treatment, please talk to your doctor first, and tell me in our first conversation. If I don’t think it’s right for you, I’ll say so.

Can it be done online?

Yes. All sessions are online. You’ll need a quiet room where you won’t be disturbed for a few hours, a good connection and headphones.
