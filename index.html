Past life regression

How it works

Between-lives regression

A longer, deeper session into what people describe as the time between one life and the next. Often a second step, once a past life has been explored.

How it works

How it works

You’ll know the shape of it before we begin.

  1. 1

    A free first conversation

    You tell me what draws you to regression. I tell you honestly whether I think it’s right for you, how a session works and what it costs. There’s no obligation.

  2. 2

    The session

    Online, from somewhere quiet at home. I guide you into a calm, focused state where you stay aware throughout. I guide; I don’t lead you towards answers.

Before you book

What people usually ask.

More questions
Do I have to believe in past lives?

No. Many people come curious rather than convinced. You don’t need any particular belief to explore, and you’re free to draw your own conclusions.

Is it safe?

You stay aware and in control throughout. You can hear everything, speak, and open your eyes whenever you like. Regression isn’t a substitute for medical or mental-health care, so if you’re under treatment, tell me in our first conversation and I’ll say honestly whether it’s right for you.

What if I don’t see anything?

It happens, and it’s fine. Some experiences are vivid, others are quiet impressions or feelings. Often more comes with a little practice and trust.

How does an online session work?

We meet on a video call, and I send you the link beforehand. You’ll need a quiet, private room where you won’t be disturbed, headphones, a steady connection, and somewhere comfortable to sit or lie back. A laptop or tablet works better than a phone.
