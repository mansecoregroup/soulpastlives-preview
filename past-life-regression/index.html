Looking back

Past life regression

A calm, guided way of exploring what may sit beyond this lifetime. No fixed beliefs required.

Regression is focused attention.

I guide you into a calm, absorbed state, close to the moment just before sleep. From there, your attention can turn inward and follow what it finds.

You stay aware and in control.

You hear everything, you can speak, and you can stop whenever you like. Nothing happens that you don’t choose.

I don’t tell you what to believe.

Some people experience what emerges as memory from another life. Others see it as metaphor, imagination or something they can’t yet name. I don’t lead you towards any answer.

What emerges is yours to interpret.

Afterwards we talk it through, at your pace. The meaning you give it is your own.

What people ask.

Do I have to believe in past lives?

No. Many people come curious rather than convinced. You don’t need any particular belief to explore, and you’re free to draw your own conclusions.

Will I be asleep?

No. Most people feel deeply relaxed but aware throughout. You’ll remember the session, and you can open your eyes at any point.

What if nothing comes up?

It happens, and it’s fine. Experiences vary: some are vivid, some are quiet impressions. Often more comes with a little practice and trust.

Is it right for everyone?

Not always. Regression isn’t a substitute for medical or mental-health care. If you have a diagnosed condition or are under treatment, please talk to your doctor first, and tell me in our first conversation. If I don’t think it’s right for you, I’ll say so.

What about the time between lives?

That’s a longer, deeper session of its own. You can read about it on the between-lives regression page.

Can it be done online?

Yes, all sessions are online. You’ll need a quiet, private room, headphones and a steady connection. See what you need for an online session.

How long does a session take?

Long enough not to rush. We’ll agree the shape of it in your first conversation, before you commit to anything.
