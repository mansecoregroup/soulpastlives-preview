Privacy and cookies

Last updated 13 November 2026

Who looks after your information

This website belongs to Carl Dourish. If you have a question about your information, email info@themansegroup.com.

What I collect

When you book a conversation or email me, I receive what you choose to send: usually your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and what you’d like to talk about.

Booking a time

If you choose a time for a conversation, you book it on Cal.com, a separate scheduling service. Cal.com receives the details you enter there, such as your name, email address and the time you choose, and handles them under its own privacy policy. Cal.com may also use cookies on its own pages. If our call uses Cal.com’s own video service, Cal Video, that is covered by Cal.com’s policy too.

How I use it

Only to reply to you and, if we work together, to arrange and carry out that work. I don’t sell or share your information for marketing.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to working together are deleted within twelve months. If we work together, I keep records only as long as needed for the work and any legal requirements.

Cookies

This site doesn’t set any cookies. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn’t load anything from other companies’ servers, so there’s nothing to accept or decline.

Links to other sites, such as Cal.com for booking, Payhip for the guided audios and Lulu for the books, take you to sites with their own cookie and privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time by emailing me.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change with it.
