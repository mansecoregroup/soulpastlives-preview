Terms

Last updated 13 November 2026

The first conversation

The first conversation is free and carries no obligation. In it we agree whether regression feels right for you, how many sessions it’s likely to take, and what it will cost.

Booking and payment

Once you decide to go ahead, I’ll send you the date, the fee and a way to pay. A session is booked once payment is received, before the session takes place.

Changing or cancelling

If you need to move or cancel a session, please let me know at least 48 hours beforehand and I’ll rebook it or refund you in full.

With less than 48 hours’ notice, or if you don’t join the session, the fee is normally kept. If something truly unavoidable happens, tell me and I’ll be fair about it.

If I ever need to cancel, you can choose a new date or a full refund.

Online sessions

All sessions take place online. Please join from a quiet, private place where you won’t be disturbed, with a reliable connection, and never while driving. If the connection fails and we can’t continue, we’ll finish the session at another time at no extra cost.

Who sessions are for

Sessions are for adults aged 18 and over.

Regression is an exploration. It isn’t medical or psychological treatment, and it isn’t a substitute for care from a doctor or mental-health professional. If you’re receiving treatment for a mental-health condition, are pregnant, or have epilepsy, please tell me before we book. I may suggest that now isn’t the right time.

What you experience

I can’t promise what you’ll experience or what it will mean. Some people find vivid detail and others find something quieter. I don’t ask you to believe anything, and the meaning you take from a session is yours.

Confidentiality

What you share in a session stays between us. I would only break that confidence if I believed you or someone else were at serious risk of harm, or if the law required it. Sessions aren’t recorded unless we agree to it beforehand. How I handle your information is set out in the privacy notice.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, email info@themansegroup.com before you book.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top will change with them. The terms that apply to you are the ones in place when you booked.
