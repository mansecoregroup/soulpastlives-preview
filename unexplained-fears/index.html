Why people come

Unexplained fears

Fears that seem to have no beginning.

Some fears make sense. Others arrive fully formed: water, heights, a place, a sound, with nothing in your life to explain them. Regression is one way of exploring where they seem to begin.

What people tell me

“I’ve been afraid of it for as long as I can remember.”

“Nothing ever happened to cause it.”

“I know it’s irrational. That doesn’t help.”

“It feels older than me.”

How we explore it.

We begin with the fear as you know it now: when it appears, what it feels like, what you already understand about it. Then, in a calm and focused state, I guide you back towards where it seems to start.

Some people find a memory from earlier in this life. Some find something that feels as if it belongs to another time. You stay aware throughout, and what you make of it is yours to decide.

What people take from it.

  • A clearer sense of where the fear seems to come from
  • Room to see it differently
  • Your own understanding, in your own words
