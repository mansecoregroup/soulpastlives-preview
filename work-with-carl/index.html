How it works

Work with Carl

You don’t need to believe anything in particular. You need curiosity, and someone experienced to guide you.

It starts with a conversation.

You tell me what draws you to regression. I tell you honestly whether I think it’s right for you, and how a session works.

Then I guide you into regression.

A calm, focused state where you stay aware throughout. I guide; I don’t lead you towards answers.

You look for yourself.

Whatever emerges, whether vivid or quiet, familiar or surprising, is yours. Nothing is suggested to you.

Afterwards, we make sense of it together.

We talk through what you found, at your pace. The meaning you give it is your own.

You’ll know the shape of it before we begin.

How long it’s likely to take and what it costs are agreed in your first conversation. Online, wherever you are.

For your online session

What you’ll need at home.

I send you a video-call link beforehand, and we check everything together before we begin.

  • A quiet, private room

    Somewhere you won’t be disturbed or overheard for the whole session. Phones on silent, and doors closed.

  • Headphones

    So my voice is close and clear, and the room around you fades.

  • A laptop or tablet

    Propped up so I can see you, with the charger plugged in. A phone works, but a bigger screen is better.

  • A steady connection

    If the call drops and we can’t continue, we’ll finish another time at no extra cost.

  • Somewhere comfortable

    A chair you can lean back in, or a bed or sofa, with a blanket nearby. People often feel cooler as they relax.

Start with unexplained fears, recurring patterns, strong emotions, familiar connections or beyond this lifetime. Or read about past life regression and between-lives regression.

Not sure if it’s for you? That’s what the first conversation is for.
